One calendar. Every network.

post2post schedules and publishes your videos and posts to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Pinterest and Google Business Profile from one place.

How it works

  1. Connect your accounts with each platform's official login (for TikTok: Login Kit).
  2. Upload a video or image and write the caption.
  3. Pick the networks and a time on the calendar — or publish now.
  4. Track the status of every post in the dashboard.

Built for your own accounts

post2post publishes only content you upload, only to accounts you connected, and never shares your data. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
